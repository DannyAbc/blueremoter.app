Skyflow 隐私政策

版本 v1.0 · 生效日期：2026-08-16

概述

Skyflow 是一款把你的 Android 手机变成电脑蓝牙外设（鼠标、触控板、键盘、数字键盘、多媒体遥控器、PPT 翻页笔）的应用。我们高度重视你的隐私。本政策说明 Skyflow 如何处理你的数据——简而言之：Skyflow 不收集、不存储、不传输任何个人数据到任何服务器。

我们收集的信息

无。Skyflow 不收集任何个人信息、使用数据、设备标识符或分析统计数据。应用不包含任何广告、分析或追踪 SDK。

数据的本地存储

以下数据仅存储在你的设备本地，不会离开你的手机：

  • 应用设置（光标速度、目标系统、手势绑定等），通过 Android DataStore 保存；
  • 你手动选择过的蓝牙设备信息（名称与地址），用于快速重连；
  • 剪贴板历史仅临时保存在内存中，应用关闭后即清除，绝不写入磁盘。

权限说明

  • 蓝牙权限（BLUETOOTH / BLUETOOTH_CONNECT / BLUETOOTH_ADVERTISE / BLUETOOTH_SCAN）：用于与你的电脑建立蓝牙 HID 连接，这是应用的核心功能。
  • 通知权限（POST_NOTIFICATIONS）：用于显示连接状态的前台服务通知。
  • 网络状态权限（ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE）：仅用于判断设备是否联网，以便在离线时改为显示应用内置的隐私政策备份。Skyflow 本身不进行任何网络数据传输。
  • 振动权限（VIBRATE）：用于按键与连接状态的触觉反馈。

网络传输

Skyflow 的所有输入数据（鼠标移动、按键、媒体控制）仅通过蓝牙直接发送到你主动连接的电脑。应用不会将任何数据发送到互联网。

第三方服务

Skyflow 不使用任何第三方分析、广告或数据收集服务。

儿童隐私

Skyflow 不面向 13 岁以下儿童，也不会有意识地收集儿童的任何信息。

政策变更

如本政策有更新，新版本将发布在本页面并更新版本号与生效日期，应用内的离线备份也会随之更新。重大变更我们会在应用商店更新说明中提示。

Skyflow Privacy Policy

Version 1.0 · Effective: 2026-08-16

Overview

Skyflow turns your Android phone into a Bluetooth peripheral for your computer (mouse, touchpad, keyboard, numpad, media remote, and presentation clicker). We take your privacy seriously. In short: Skyflow does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to any server.

Information We Collect

None. Skyflow does not collect any personal information, usage data, device identifiers, or analytics. The app contains no advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs.

On-Device Storage

The following data is stored locally on your device only and never leaves your phone:

  • App settings (cursor speed, target OS, gesture bindings, etc.), stored via Android DataStore;
  • Bluetooth devices you have manually selected (name and address), used for quick reconnection;
  • Clipboard history is held temporarily in memory only and is cleared when the app closes; it is never written to disk.

Permissions

  • Bluetooth (BLUETOOTH / BLUETOOTH_CONNECT / BLUETOOTH_ADVERTISE / BLUETOOTH_SCAN): required to establish the Bluetooth HID connection with your computer — the core functionality of the app.
  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): used for the foreground-service notification showing connection status.
  • Network state (ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE): used solely to detect whether the device is online, so the app can fall back to the built-in offline copy of this privacy policy. Skyflow itself performs no network data transfer.
  • Vibration (VIBRATE): used for haptic feedback on key presses and connection events.

Network Transmission

All input data (cursor movement, key presses, media controls) is sent directly over Bluetooth to the computer you explicitly connected. The app never sends any data to the internet.

Third-Party Services

Skyflow does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or data-collection services.

Children's Privacy

Skyflow is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect any information from children.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy is updated, the new version will be published on this page with an updated version number and effective date, and the offline copy inside the app will be updated accordingly. Significant changes will also be noted in the app store release notes.
